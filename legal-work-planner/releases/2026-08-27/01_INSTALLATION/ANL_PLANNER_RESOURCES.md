## 0. Purpose

This annex tells the Planner **what resources exist and when they may be useful after the Open Work Scan**. It must not define PL2.

It is a compact navigation/semantic overlay, not automatically a second registry or organisational master. Where an authoritative inventory exists, reference it instead of copying dynamic truth.

## 8. Resource-map rules

1. PL2 Open Work Scan comes first.

2. MAP resources are consulted at PL4 only when relevant to the provisional work model.

3. `KNOWN_TO_EXIST` is not `ACCESSIBLE`.

4. Missing resources do not prove that a method/capability does not exist.

5. Dynamic facts should be retrieved; stable semantics may be curated.

6. No credential material.

7. Resource changes should require this annex/inventory update, not a Core edit.
