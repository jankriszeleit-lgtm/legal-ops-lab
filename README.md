# Legal Ops Lab

A public workspace for practical legal-operations experiments, reusable prompts, planning methods, templates and evaluation material.

The focus is deliberately narrow:

> publish the parts of legal operations and legal engineering that benefit from reuse, critique and collaboration.

This repository is **not** intended to publish every internal method, private workflow or proprietary knowledge asset behind the author's broader legal-engineering work.

## What belongs here

Typical material may include:

- Legal Work Planner releases;
- installation and configuration guides;
- resource and capability templates;
- practical Legal Ops experiments;
- reusable prompt components intended for public use;
- public pilot designs and evaluation material;
- examples that can be shared without confidential or client-specific information.

## What does not belong here

Do not add:

- client or matter information;
- confidential or privileged material;
- internal credentials, API keys or access tokens;
- private repository internals;
- proprietary knowledge stacks;
- personal methodology that is intentionally kept outside the public project;
- third-party material without a clear right to redistribute it.

## Current project

### Legal Work Planner

The first public project is the **Legal Work Planner**: a prompt-first method for turning an imperfect professional work request into a practical, resource-aware work plan before choosing tools or workflows.

See [`legal-work-planner/`](legal-work-planner/).
